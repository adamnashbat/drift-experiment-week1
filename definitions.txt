Data drift is the sudden or gradual change in the distribution of data over time. where the relationship between
the input variable (X) and the target variable (Y) remains stable (unlike concept drift). This change in distribution
is commonly caused by a multitude of factors, including changes in the data source, economic shifts / market trends, and poor preprocessing of the data.
Data Drift significantly hinders the performance of machine learning models and declines their accuracy causing them
to be ineffective in the real world. Overall, while data drift can sometimes be unpredictable, there are solutions to mitigate its effects.
